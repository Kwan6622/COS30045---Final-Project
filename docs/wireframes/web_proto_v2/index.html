Opening: "Every day, 120 hospital beds…" - unit pictogram

One icon = one hospital bed filled every day, 2011-2021 (bed days ÷ 4,018 days). v2: cyclist beds striped, 2 keyboard stops, table.

Redraws on: width, wardPlayed, theme, cvd

  1. 1 Icons fill in one by one the first time the opening is on screen (instant with reduced motion)
  2. 2 v2 Tab = one stop per road user (not 120 icons); focus dims the other group, tooltip + announcer
  3. 3 v2 table: road user, beds per day, days in hospital
  4. 4 v2 cyclists striped, pedestrians solid: readable without colour
  5. 5 <desc>: 120 beds, 65 pedestrians, 55 cyclists, and why (longer stays)

Chapter 1 "Who": scrollytelling line chart

Admissions per year by road user, 2011-2021; default = change since 2012 (2012 = 100). v2: shapes per road user, year-by-year keyboard stops.

Redraws on: trendMode, trendFocus, width, theme, cvd

  1. 1 v2 yellow rings with dark edge (passes 2:1 on white); labels give the 2012->19 change
  2. 2 Chips: index (growth) or counts; v2 announced to screen readers
  3. 3 Direct labels with v2 shapes: ● ped ■ cyc ▲ moto ◆ car (dashed)
  4. 4 Each card sets trendMode / trendFocus; other lines fade to 18%
  5. 5 v2 Tab, then ← → = one year at a time: crosshair + tooltip for all road users (same as mouse hover)
  6. 6 v2 table: year x road user, follows the chosen scale

Chapter 4 "When & where" (a): month of the year

Each month ÷ the average month (1.0 = typical), 2011-2019. v2: circles + solid (ped), squares + long dash (cyc), month stops, table.

Redraws on: width, theme, cvd (static data)

  1. 1 Yellow rings (v2 dark edge): each group's peak month (cyclists March, pedestrians May, June about equal)
  2. 2 Winter shaded: pedestrians stay high, cyclists drop
  3. 3 v2 Tab, then ← →: one month at a time, both groups read together
  4. 4 v2 table: month x road user, index and admissions
  5. 5 v2 two lines differ by shape AND dash, not only colour (W8 s.26)
  6. 6 v2 text fix: v1 said the peak is June; the data peak is May (+14.9% vs +14.7%): text now says May-June

Chapter 4 "When & where" (b): states, linked views

Equal-area tile map per 100,000 people (5-step ramp); Enter / click on a state updates the line chart. v2: teaser, take-away, keyboard, table.

Redraws on: mapGroup, mapState, width, theme, cvd

  1. 1 v2 prompt answers the first half (NT) and teases the second
  2. 2 Tiles = one Tab stop; v2 ← → move between tiles, Enter selects (focus stays on the tile after the redraw)
  3. 3 v2 take-away line names the highest and lowest state
  4. 4 Linked line chart: selected state (solid) vs Australia (dashed); v2 role=img + <title>/<desc>
  5. 5 v2 table: every state, highest first, plus Australia
  6. 6 Change of state or road user is announced (v2 live region)

Chapter 5 "What you can do": role picker + action panel

The reader picks how they use the road; the panel repeats the relevant numbers and lists sourced actions.
